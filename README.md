### 👋 About me

I'm a synthetic inorganic materials scientist at **Swansea University, UK**. I design noble-metal and multimetallic nanomaterials and turn them into real applications, from **early-stage cancer diagnosis** to **removing persistent pollutants from wastewater**.

### 🔬 Research focus

- 🧬 **Biomedical nanomaterials & sensing:** Ag@Au nanostars and magnetic nanoparticles for a pancreatic-cancer nanosensor; antimicrobial and anticancer trimetallic nanofluids
- 💧 **Electrochemical & plasma water treatment:** nanostructured anodes, cold plasma and ozonation to degrade PFOA and antibiotics
- ⚗️ **Green & microwave-assisted synthesis:** fast, energy-efficient routes to trimetallic nanoparticles (granted Indian patent)
- 🔋 **Functional & energy materials:** high-entropy oxide electrocatalysts, humidity sensors and alloys
